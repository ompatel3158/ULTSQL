Request Account & Data Deletion

Step-by-step instructions to request account and personal data deletion for ULTSQL.

APPLICATION: ULTSQL - Database Studio & AI (com.ultsql.app)
DEVELOPER: Om Patel (ULTSQL Engineering)
DOMAIN: ultsql.tech
IN-APP ACTION

⚡ Instant Deletion

Execute instant account deletion and cloud association purge directly in the mobile app via Settings.

DIRECT SUPPORT

✉️ Web & Email Processing

Submit requests by email for uninstalled devices. All requests processed within 48 hours guaranteed.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

Users of ULTSQL have complete sovereignty and control over their account data and local database storage. You can delete your account and associated personal data at any time using either of the following methods:

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Fastest)

  1. Open the ULTSQL app on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to Settings > Personalization / Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and select Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your authentication records and cloud sync associations are deleted immediately.

Method 2: Web or Email Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit a deletion request directly to our engineering team:

  • Email: support@ultsql.tech
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request - ULTSQL
  • Message Content: Please include the email address associated with your ULTSQL account.

We process all email requests within 48 hours.

What Data is Deleted

  • Account Authentication: Your email address, profile name, and unique authentication ID stored in Firebase Authentication / Supabase are permanently deleted.
  • Cloud Sync Tokens: All cloud synchronization tokens, device presence records, and remote database relay configurations are permanently purged.

What Data is Kept

None. We do not retain any residual personal or authentication data once your deletion request is processed.

Retention Period

All remote personal and account records are deleted immediately upon in-app confirmation, or within 48 hours of an email request. There is no waiting retention period.

Local On-Device Database Storage

Because ULTSQL is a local-first application, your relational tables, NoSQL collections, and vector search embeddings reside in sandboxed local device storage. To erase all local database files from your device:

  1. Open Android Settings > Apps > ULTSQL.
  2. Tap Storage & cache.
  3. Tap Clear storage / Clear data.
