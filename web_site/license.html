What is the ULTSQL license? ▾
ULTSQL is licensed under the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, MIT Future License (FSL-1.1-MIT). It is a modern source-available standard (stewarded by Sentry) that gives you full access to the source code, allows free commercial embedding and SaaS use, and automatically converts to the standard open-source MIT License after 2 years.
Can I use ULTSQL for free in my commercial application? ▾
Yes. You can build, ship, and sell commercial applications and SaaS products that embed ULTSQL without paying any licensing fees or royalties.
Do I need to show “Powered by ULTSQL” or any forced attribution? ▾
No. Under FSL-1.1-MIT, there is no forced UI attribution or badge requirement. You only need to keep the standard copyright notice in source distributions as customary.
Do I have to open-source my own application? ▾
No. FSL-1.1-MIT does not require your application to become open source. You can keep your own application's code 100% proprietary.
Can I use ULTSQL in a SaaS application? ▾
Yes. For example, you can build an accounting SaaS, an analytics platform, or an AI application powered by ULTSQL. That is fully permitted because ULTSQL is used to power your software, not sold as a raw database hosting service.
Can I run ULTSQL on AWS, GCP, or Azure for my company? ▾
Yes. Internal and customer-facing application deployment is permitted on any cloud provider. The only restriction is offering ULTSQL itself as a commercial hosted DBaaS product to third parties in competition with the author.
When does it convert to the MIT License? ▾
Exactly two years after release, each version automatically converts into the standard, permissive MIT License with no further restrictions.
