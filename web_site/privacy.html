1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to ULTSQL Studio, accessible at ultsql.tech and via the official mobile application (com.ultsql.app) distributed on the Google Play Store. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent data practices, detailing what information we process, how it is handled, and the strict controls you retain over your personal and database assets.

2. Local-First Database Architecture

ULTSQL Studio is engineered with an uncompromising local-first philosophy. When you create relational SQL tables, execute queries, store NoSQL document collections, or generate high-dimensional vector embeddings, this data is saved directly in the sandboxed local storage of your device.

  • No Telemetry on Data: We never log, collect, inspect, or transmit the contents of your local database tables to our servers.
  • Offline Capability: Core database features operate fully offline without requiring any active internet connection.

3. Specific Permissions & Data We Process

A. Microphone & Audio (Voice Queries for Sofy AI)

When you activate the microphone button in the Sofy AI chat interface, the app requests permission to record audio (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO).

  • Purpose: To transcribe spoken natural language prompts (e.g., "Show me all tables ordered by date") into text for database operations.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Audio captures are converted to text and immediately discarded. Audio files are never permanently retained on external servers, are never used for voice profiling, and are never shared with third-party advertisers.
  • Granular Control: Microphone access is strictly optional. You can deny microphone access and use the text-based chat interface at all times.

B. Storage & File Management

The app interacts with device storage strictly to fulfill user-initiated actions:

  • Exporting database backups (SQL dump files, CSV tables, JSON records).
  • Importing existing database files or schema definitions that you explicitly select.

C. User Account Information (Optional)

If you choose to create an account or authenticate using Google Sign-In:

  • We process basic profile details (email address, display name, and unique authentication identifier) via Firebase Authentication and Supabase.
  • This is used solely to authenticate your session and enable optional peer-to-peer cloud collaboration and database synchronization across your personal devices.

D. AI Assistant Interactions

When you submit questions to the Sofy AI assistant, the text prompt and minimal table schema metadata necessary to formulate the SQL query are transmitted securely via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS to authorized inference engines (OpenRouter or Google Gemini API). Your queries are not used to train public foundation models.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure

To ensure dependable synchronization and authentication, ULTSQL Studio integrates with trusted developer infrastructure:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase: Authentication, Google Play licensing, and application distribution. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase: Scalable backend infrastructure for optional cloud synchronization. (Supabase Privacy Policy)

5. Security Safeguards

We employ industry-leading protocols to protect your information:

  • End-to-End Transport Security: All remote communications (authentication, AI generation, Forge cloud sync) are strictly enforced over encrypted TLS 1.3 / HTTPS channels.
  • Sandboxed Android Storage: Application databases are stored in private internal Android storage, inaccessible to other apps without root permissions.
  • Secure Key Vaults: User-supplied API keys for AI providers remain encrypted in local device storage.

6. Your Data Rights & Deletion Options

You maintain complete sovereignty over your information:

  • Local Data Wipe: You can completely erase all local tables, databases, and cached data at any time through Android Settings > Apps > ULTSQL > Storage > Clear Storage.
  • Account Deletion: You can request full account deletion inside the App Settings or by contacting our engineering team. Upon request, all remote authentication credentials and sync records are irreversibly deleted.

7. Children's Privacy

ULTSQL Studio is designed for developers, students, and database administrators. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
