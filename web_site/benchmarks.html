Performance Benchmarks

Measured throughput and latency benchmarks comparing UltSQL (100% Pure Dart) against standard C embedded databases.

ℹ️ Tested Benchmark Hardware Notice
Tested benchmark throughput (e.g. 1,200,000+ rows/sec in-memory throughput) was measured on an ASUS ROG Strix G16 (2023) powered by an Intel Core i7-13650HX, 16GB DDR5 4800MT/s RAM, 1TB Gen 5 NVMe SSD, and NVIDIA RTX 4050 6GB. Actual benchmark speeds may vary higher or lower depending on your target device hardware, storage controller, and CPU specifications.
