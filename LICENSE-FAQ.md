# ULTSQL License FAQ

## What is the ULTSQL license?

ULTSQL uses a **source-available license**.

It allows developers and companies to use, modify, distribute, and commercially use ULTSQL without paying royalties for permitted uses.

However, some uses—particularly offering ULTSQL itself as a commercial hosted database service—require a separate commercial license.

## Is ULTSQL open source?

ULTSQL is **source available**, but this license should not be marketed as an OSI-approved open-source license.

The source code can be inspected, modified, and used under the permissions granted by the ULTSQL license.

The license contains additional restrictions that distinguish it from conventional permissive open-source licenses.

## Can I use ULTSQL for free?

Yes.

Permitted use of ULTSQL is royalty-free under the license.

You can use it for:

- learning;
- education;
- research;
- personal projects;
- internal company projects;
- commercial applications;
- desktop software;
- mobile applications;
- embedded software; and
- SaaS applications that use ULTSQL as part of a larger product.

## Can I use ULTSQL in a commercial application?

Yes.

You can build and sell a commercial application that uses ULTSQL without paying a separate license fee, provided you follow the license terms.

You must provide the required ULTSQL attribution.

## Can I sell software containing ULTSQL?

Yes.

The license does not prevent you from selling software that incorporates ULTSQL.

You may charge your customers for your own product.

## Do I have to open-source my application?

No.

This license does not require your entire application to become open source.

You can keep your own application's source code proprietary.

You only need to comply with the conditions that apply to your use and distribution of ULTSQL.

## Do I have to show “Powered by ULTSQL”?

Yes, when distributing an application or product that incorporates ULTSQL.

The required attribution is:

**“Powered by ULTSQL”**

It should appear in an appropriate location such as an About, Legal, Licenses, Credits, or Documentation section.

## Can I remove the ULTSQL attribution?

No.

Removing required attribution violates the license.

## Can I modify ULTSQL?

Yes.

You can modify the source code and create derivative versions.

Your modifications remain subject to the applicable requirements of the ULTSQL license when you distribute them.

## Can I sell my modified version?

Yes, provided that the modified version complies with the license.

You may charge money for your software.

However, you cannot use the modified version to provide a prohibited Commercial Cloud Service without the required commercial license.

## Can I use ULTSQL on AWS?

Yes.

You can run ULTSQL on AWS or another cloud provider for your own application, company, development environment, testing, or internal infrastructure.

The restriction is not about **where** ULTSQL runs.

It is about **offering ULTSQL itself as a commercial hosted database service to customers**.

## Can I use ULTSQL in a SaaS application?

Yes.

For example, you can build:

> An accounting SaaS → powered by ULTSQL

or:

> An analytics SaaS → powered by ULTSQL

or:

> A project-management SaaS → powered by ULTSQL

That is permitted because ULTSQL is being used as part of a larger application.

## Can I build “ULTSQL Cloud”?

Not under the free license if the service provides ULTSQL itself as a commercial managed database service.

A separate commercial license would be required.

## Can AWS, Google Cloud, or Azure offer ULTSQL as a managed database?

Not under the free license when the offering constitutes a Commercial Cloud Service covered by Section 5.

The provider would need a separate written commercial license from the ULTSQL Licensor.

## Why does ULTSQL restrict cloud hosting?

The restriction protects the project's ability to build its own hosted database business.

Without such a restriction, a cloud provider could potentially take the database engine, deploy it at scale, and sell it as a managed database product without contributing financially to the project.

## Do I need a commercial license to use ULTSQL inside my company's cloud infrastructure?

No.

Internal cloud deployment is permitted.

For example, a company can deploy ULTSQL on its AWS infrastructure to operate its own application.

## Do I need to pay ULTSQL royalties?

No royalties are required for permitted uses under the free license.

A separate commercial license may be required for prohibited commercial cloud-hosting uses.

## Can I redistribute ULTSQL?

Yes.

You may redistribute the source code and permitted binaries, provided that you comply with the license requirements.

## Can I include ULTSQL inside another application?

Yes.

ULTSQL may be bundled into applications and products.

The required attribution must be provided.

## Can I create a fork of ULTSQL?

Yes.

You may create modified versions and forks.

However, you should not present your fork as the official ULTSQL project or imply that it is officially maintained or endorsed by the ULTSQL Authors.

## Can I use the ULTSQL name for my fork?

The software license does not automatically grant trademark rights.

You should use your own name and branding for an independent fork unless you have permission to use ULTSQL trademarks.

## Does the license give me ownership of ULTSQL?

No.

The license gives you specific rights to use the software.

It does not transfer copyright ownership.

## What happens if I violate the license?

Your license rights can terminate if you materially violate the terms.

The license provides a cure period for certain violations after written notice.

## Can ULTSQL change its license later?

The ULTSQL project may release future versions under different licensing terms.

However, rights already granted to existing recipients under an applicable license should be evaluated according to the terms of that license.

## Is ULTSQL free for students?

Yes.

Educational and research use is permitted without a license fee.

## Is ULTSQL free for startups?

Yes, for permitted uses.

A startup can use ULTSQL in its application or internal infrastructure without paying royalties, subject to the attribution and other license requirements.

A startup offering ULTSQL itself as a commercial managed database service would require a separate commercial license.

## What if I am unsure whether my use requires a commercial license?

Contact the ULTSQL maintainers before launching the service.

Describe:

1. how ULTSQL is being used;
2. whether customers directly access the database;
3. whether the database itself is being sold or hosted as a service; and
4. how the service is monetized.

The ULTSQL maintainers can then determine whether a separate commercial license is required.

## Where can I get a commercial cloud license?

Commercial licensing should be handled directly through the official ULTSQL project.

The commercial agreement can define:

- permitted services;
- deployment scope;
- customer limits;
- duration;
- pricing;
- support;
- branding;
- enterprise requirements; and
- other commercial conditions.

## What is the simplest way to understand the license?

Think of it this way:

**Use ULTSQL → Free**

**Modify ULTSQL → Free**

**Build an application with ULTSQL → Free**

**Sell that application → Allowed**

**Run ULTSQL internally on the cloud → Allowed**

**Offer ULTSQL itself as a paid managed database → Commercial license required**

**Remove required attribution → Not allowed**
